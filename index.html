// our first event · 14.11.2026

Robot Fight Night

On Saturday, 14 November 2026, two Unitree G1 humanoid robots met in a show match at W3 Hub in Berlin-Kreuzberg, each one for a Berlin tech startup. It ran like a sports event, with rounds, a referee and judges.

The evening was part of the after party of Unicon 2026 and the Entropy Alignment & Security Hackathon. For us it was a way to show what humanoid robots can do today, outside a lab and up close.

// about

Robots up close

Most people only know humanoid robots from videos. We want them in front of people: at events, at the workbench, with students. Machine Native is a grassroots club, open to anyone who's curious.

01

Learning

We bring students and anyone curious to real robots. You see how they're controlled and what they still can't do.

02

Club

Anyone can join. Some of us build, some write code, some run events. You don't need any experience.

03

Events

We show robots where people are. Our first event was on 14 November 2026, the next one is in planning.

Machine Native is independent and works with CODE University of Applied Sciences Berlin. Machine Native was initiated by Jannik Fuchs and Pascal Finn Krebs.

Questions

What is Machine Native?

A robotics club from Berlin. We bring students and anyone curious to real humanoid robots, and we put on public robot events.

Who is behind Machine Native?

The club was initiated by Jannik Fuchs and Pascal Finn Krebs.

Where did Robot Fight Night take place?

At W3 Hub in Berlin-Kreuzberg on 14 November 2026, at the after party of Unicon 2026 and the Entropy Alignment & Security Hackathon. Two humanoid robots met in a show boxing match.

Is Machine Native part of CODE University?

No. We are independent and work with CODE University of Applied Sciences Berlin.

Will there be more events?

Yes, the next one is in planning. Sign up in the events section and we'll email you the date.

Can I use footage from Robot Fight Night?

The recording is licensed to third parties, so please link to it. Press photos are in the press section. For anything else, write to pascalfinn.krebs@code.berlin.

How do I reach you?
